INTRODUCTION TO ARM7 ASSEMBLY LANGUAGE
PROGRAMMING (DATA PROCESSING-II)
Aim: To understand data processing (Arithmetic operations)
1.Program with different arithmetic instructions.
01 AREA PROGRAM1, CODE, READONLY;
02 ENTRY
03 LDR R1,NUM1
04 LDR R2,NUM2
05 LDR RO,=RES
06 ADD R3,R1,R2 ;R3=R1+R2
07 STR R3, [RO], #4
08 ADC R3,R1,R2 #R3=R1+R2+C
09 STR R3, [RO], #4
10 SUB R3,R1,R2 -R3=Ri-R2
nN STR R3, [RO], #4
12 SBC R3,R1,R2 7R3=R1-R2+C
13 STR R3, [RO], #4
14 RSB R3,R1,R2 ;R3=R2-R1
15 STR R3, [RO], #4
16 RSC R3,R1,R2 9 ;R3=R2-R1+C-1
7 STR R3, [RO], #4
18 BIC R3,R1,R2 ;R3=R1 AND (~R2)
19 STR R3, [RO]
20 Ss BS
21 NUM1 DCD OXF7654321
22 NUM2 DCD OXF2345678
23 AREA MEMORY, DATA, READWRITE ;?
24 RES DCD 0 ; starting address is 0x40000000
25 END
Note: Include S at the end in ADD, ADC, SUB, SBC,RSB, RSC and BIC of the above program
and observe the CPSR register and data memory.
